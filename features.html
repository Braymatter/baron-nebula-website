Features

Everything you build, and everything you break

Every star you take gets one building, and every building can be pushed further. Here is each piece of the game, doing its job.

Buildings Refits and abilities Fleets The nebula

Buildings

One star, one building

Each star holds a single building, so every star you take asks the same question: what is this one for?

Building

Market

A Market earns credits every second, and more for every neighbouring star your team holds that has no Market of its own. Its haulers ply those trade links, and if the star falls, the attacker sacks its share of your bank.

Building

Droneyard

Pick a ship type and a Droneyard builds it for as long as you pay its upkeep. Refit one as a Manufacturing Control, add its Rally Point, and new ships from it and its neighbours march to the front in groups on their own.

Building

Fortress

A Fortress hardens the ships docked under it and shells every hostile fleet in the arc you aim, on any lane, slowing it to a crawl. Big, slow armies bleed crossing it. Bring Bombers, or a fast vanguard to take the star.

Building

Sensor Array

The nebula is dark beyond your stars. A Sensor Array lights a cone you aim, showing exactly what moves inside it, and your team's fleets fight stronger there.

Refits and abilities

Push every building further

Every building takes one refit, one of two, and a refitted building can be raised once more to a third tier that gives it an ability.

Refits

Refits

Foundry or Manufacturing Control for a Droneyard, Bastion or Battery for a Fortress, Long-range or Uplink for a Sensor Array, Defense Industries or Vault for a Market. Each deepens its star's job one way, and it can't be changed once fitted.

Ability · Battery Fortress

EMP Strike

A Battery hurls a bolt at an enemy star in reach, and every building there goes dark for a while: no shells, no ships, no income. Then send the fleet in.

Ability · Uplink Sensor Array

Tachyon Lance

An Uplink fires a beam from its star in any direction you aim, straight across the map and every lane in its way. Everyone sees the warning, then it burns every fleet caught in its path, yours too, ship by ship. Garrisons sit untouched; anything that flies out keeps what's left.

Ability · Defense Industries Market

Hired Help

Call in a company of Interceptors, Bombers and Frigates. Moments later it lands at any star of yours, straight into the fight there, and stays yours for good.

Abilities · Foundry, Vault, Bastion, Long-range

Boosts

Overtime runs every Droneyard you own at triple speed. Market Boost, Fortress Boost and Deep Survey do the same for your Markets, Fortresses and Sensor cones, all of them at once, for a while.

Fleets

Drag, send, no recall

Ships are free; the Droneyards that build them aren't. What you do with them decides the match.

Orders

Sending fleets

Drag from your star to any other and let go: the fleet finds its way down the hyperlanes. Circle several stars to send from all of them. A sent fleet can't be called back, and fights to the end.

Ship types

Ships

Fast Interceptors hunt Bombers. Bombers break Frigates and Fortresses. Tough Frigates shred Interceptors. A fleet flies at its slowest ship's speed, so send the fast ones ahead to strike first.

Sieges

Sieges

Beat a star's defenders and the siege begins: your fleet tears down its buildings, then the star itself, and must hold on until it falls. Sack a Market and its share of their bank is yours. Relieve a siege in time and the buildings survive.

The nebula

A map that fights back

Every match is a new nebula, and it is never only stars and lanes.

Map feature

Wormholes

A wormhole joins two distant stars. Fleets cross its line at one speed whatever their ships, and enemies meeting head-on inside destroy each other ship for ship. Every fleet that dives in might shut it for a while.

Map feature

Farcasters

A ring gate in place of a star. Hold it and it warps every ship there, allies' too, to any star in reach, priced by ships and distance. Its charge is seen by everyone, so the target gets a few seconds to brace.

Map feature

Leviathans

A colossal ship you capture like a star. It docks at each berth on its route and throws out lanes, then sails on to the next, carrying everything aboard, a fight included. The fog never hides where it is.

Upgrade

Supernova

Build a Supernova on one of your stars, in sight of everyone. Once it's done the countdown can't be stopped: the blast wipes out every ship close by, anyone's, every building in the nebula goes dark, and a black hole is left behind.

Modifiers

Twists and grants

Every few minutes the rules shift. Twists hit everyone at once, from meteor storms and comets to ion storms and collapsing lanes. In grant rounds each team votes on a card, from a war chest to a free wormhole jump.
