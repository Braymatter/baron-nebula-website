Description

Baron Nebula is a real-time space strategy game for one to twelve players. Each match is played on a nebula of stars joined by hyperlanes. Every star you hold takes one upgrade, a Market, Shipyard, Fortress or Sensor Array, so each star's role is a choice. You drag fleets along the lanes, and take a star by beating its defenders and grinding down what's built on it. A match lasts about twenty minutes.

Every five minutes a modifier enters the match, from orbital strikes and beam weapons to a mercenary company auctioned to the highest bidder. Leviathans, colossal ships on fixed routes, dock between stars and throw out hyperlanes of their own; capture one and you can warp its garrison behind enemy lines.

It plays solo against bots that play under the same fog of war as you, or online, free-for-all or in teams, on servers that run every match.

Features

  • Send a fleet by dragging from a star; read a battle at a glance.
  • Generated maps of six kinds, from duels to twelve players, with wormholes, and a map editor whose maps share as a code.
  • Sieges: taking a star means breaking its defences, so a relief fleet can still save it.
  • Timed modifiers, drafted, voted on or drawn at random.
  • Leviathans: moving stars to fight over, with a warp drive.
  • Online play with lobbies, teams, spectators and ratings; bots at three difficulties; a tutorial.
