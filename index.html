Features

Easy to read, hard to master

A new nebula every match

Six kinds of generated map, from duels to twelve-player brawls, with wormholes that cut across them and an editor for building and sharing your own.

Leviathans NEW

Colossal ships cruise set routes, docking to throw hyperlanes out to the stars nearby. Take one and warp its garrison behind enemy lines.

Battles you can read at a glance

Fleets swarm, sieges grind down a star's defences, and a Fortress bombards anything in its arc. You always know who's winning.

No two matches play the same

Every five minutes a modifier enters the match: orbital strikes, beam weapons, wormhole drives, or a mercenary company auctioned to the highest bidder.
