Features

Easy to read, hard to master
01

Conquer the stars

Send your fleets down the hyperlanes and break the garrison. Every star you take pays you and opens new lanes.

02

Build your forces

Fortify the front. Fortresses shell anything in their arc, and Sensor Arrays see deep into the fog.

03

Outgun and Outthink the Enemy

Pull back the fog with a Deep Survey, black out their defences with an EMP Strike, then warp a fleet straight in through a Farcaster.

04

Drop in for a visit

Wormholes cut across the map. Go in at the front and come out deep behind their lines.

05

Or send something nice

Load your fleets aboard a Leviathan and let the colossal ship carry them into enemy space.

06

Dominate the Nebula

Push the front back star by star until the map is yours. Every match is a new nebula, for up to twelve players, against bots or online.

07

Or Burn it Down

Can't hold a star? Set it off as a supernova: every ship there dies, and every structure in the nebula goes dark.
